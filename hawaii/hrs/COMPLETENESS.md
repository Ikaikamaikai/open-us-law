# HRS completeness check

This compares the HRS sections in this database (Open US Law snapshot) with two official documents:

- **Hawaii Revised Statutes, Table of Contents**, updated 2/10/2025 ([source/hrstoc.pdf](source/hrstoc.pdf))
- **Table of Disposition, Table 2: Session Laws of 2025 to HRS** ([source/tablesofdisposition_2025.pdf](source/tablesofdisposition_2025.pdf))
- **The Legislature's own list of section pages** on data.capitol.hawaii.gov ([server_sections.csv](server_sections.csv), made by `hrs/fill_missing.py --all`)

Generated by `hrs/check.py`. Each list below is also a CSV in [checks/](checks/).
