      About Hawaii Public Info

      Hawaii Public Info collects the published law of Hawaii and the United States, the parts of government and the laws that create them, and the public records you add, and lets you search all of it on your own computer. It shows each text as published and links to where it came from. It does not give legal advice, characterize anything, or decide anything for you.

      Two ways to open it

      1. This file by itself

      Double-click it. The Shape, this page, and a search of the Shape work with no program and no internet.

      2. With the search engine: everything

      1. Install Python 3 from python.org (nothing else is needed to search).
      2. Put the database at hawaii/data/hawaii.db (from the database download), or build it once: pip install duckdb pdfplumber, then python3 fetch.py and python3 build.py in the hawaii folder.
      3. In the hawaii folder run python3 hi.py serve (on Windows: py hi.py serve).
      4. Open http://127.0.0.1:8808. It answers only this computer.

      The same engine answers in the terminal (python3 hi.py HRS 92F-12) and to AI apps that speak MCP (python3 hi.py mcp; see README.md).

      How it is designed, and why

      IdeaWhere you see it
      One box (the first Google page; command palettes like Spotlight and Raycast)The first screen is one field. Citations, questions and plain words all go in it.
      Local-first software (Ink & Switch, 2019)The data and the engine are on your computer. No account, no cloud, no API key; it keeps working offline.
      Show the source (provenance)Every passage links to its official publisher. Anything parsed says how it was checked, like ✓ quote found in the law.
      Say how a question was readThe line under the box shows how your words were understood, so nothing is guessed behind your back.
      Honest empty statesWhen something is not here, it says so and where it is published, instead of showing something close.
      Progressive disclosureDetails open only when you ask: results, then a section, then what cites it.
      Recognition over recall (Nielsen)Examples under the box; the shape of everything is a list you can open, not something to remember.
      Accessible by default (WCAG 2.2 AA)Everything works from the keyboard; focus is always visible; targets are at least 24 px; status is shown by symbol and word, not color alone; screen readers hear results as they change.
      Calm technology (Weiser and Brown; Amber Case)No ads, banners, pop-ups, notifications or tracking. Motion only shows where something went, and stops if your system asks for less motion.
      Plain languageShort labels and sentences. It says what it is not.
      Your system's settingsLight or dark follows your system; the switch remembers your choice on this computer only. Text sizes follow your browser's zoom.
      Type made for draftingDrafting Mono (Owen Earl, indestructible type*, SIL Open Font License 1.1), a typewriter rhythm for working documents, set at a reading measure of at most 76 characters. The name is set in Lab Mono when its font file is in fonts/.
      One file, no dependenciesNo framework, no font or script from the internet. The page opens the same way in ten years.
      Print and copyA section prints cleanly with its source address; copy buttons include the citation and source.

      Compared with the paid service it was modeled on

      Vaquill sells a legal API and publishes the open dataset most of this law comes from. Its 30-path API, read from its published OpenAPI file (api.vaquill.ai/external/openapi.json, October 2026), lines up with this tool as follows:
